Tuning loopone pass per change, repeated

1 Change one thing On one panel, change one setting and press that tool's Save. Two changes at once cannot be told apart.

2 Run the test On C1. The line under the button is the exact command it will run with every saved setting.

3 Read three bars U2 below 1 on the blind period, overfit ratio at or below 1.1, and 60% of folds beating a constant guess.

4 Compare The record line says whether this run beat the best on file. A gain under 0.0021 is noise.

5 Keep or revert Keep the setting, or press Load best as defaults to return to the best fit on record.

Retuning rules Never tune on the blind period, since a setting chosen by looking at it has spent it. Trust only walk-forward regimes, judge on a full blind year, and rerun on names the model has not seen.
