Round-trip cost exceeds the measured edge  A basis point is a hundredth of one per cent. The best crypto edge is five to twelve of them smaller than the fifteen to twenty a round trip costs. Nothing tried has closed it.

A2Label geometrytriple barrier

The label is a modelling decision, not an observed quantity. Three barriers are set at entry: a take-profit, a stop, and a holding-period limit. Whichever the path touches first determines the class, so the same price series yields different labels under different barrier geometry.

Barrier expectancy is negative before fitting  On the 4-hour panel the profit line is reached first on 254 bars in every thousand. Aiming twice as far up as down, you need 333 to come out level. The arithmetic is losing before anything is fitted, so the first thing to check is always the target's own record, not the model's.

A3Acceptance criteria60% of folds

A pooled total can be carried by a single favourable regime, so the sample is partitioned into half-year folds and the edge must hold in 60 per cent of them, on two criteria.

B2Point-in-time screenfour criteria

Four criteria, applied conjunctively. Membership is recomputed at every bar from information available at that bar, so the screen is point-in-time rather than retrospective.

DoorBar it has to clear
Trading enough≥ 30m USDT in 24h
Moving enoughATR 2.5–12% of price
Cheap to cross≤ 0.5% estimated
Old enough≥ 120 days lived

ATR, the average true range, is how far price usually travels in a day, as a share of price. The band shown is daily; a 4-hour bar wants 1.0 to 4.9%.

The spread, the difference between the best bid and best ask, is paid on entry and exit. The archive carries no order book, so it is estimated from each day's high and low by the Corwin-Schultz estimator and gated at 0.5%. The live screen reads the quoted spread and gates at 0.05%.

Scored across the market, traded on a subset  The full universe is ranked; only assets with positive out-of-sample contribution are admitted.

B3Cross-sectional ranksurviving signal

Time-series direction, the probability an asset rises, failed on every frame. Cross-sectional rank, the ordering of assets each bar, did not.

Signal confirmed, execution rule rejected  The ranking held across train and test. The one execution rule built on it, gated on a rising bitcoin trend, passed 27 per cent of folds against 60. Untested: longer holds, and the same ranking on equities.
Tercile width approximates the universe  Measured 8 September: 266 assets at the median bar, so the top tercile is 89 names, on a stale comment claiming 5 to 7 per bar.

Crossover suppression near the zero line  Crossovers near zero fire on noise. A cross is suppressed unless it clears a band set at half the rolling standard deviation of the histogram, then holds sign for a further bar.

MACD is the 12-period less the 26-period exponential moving average, against its own 9-period average.

Illustrative, not a trading signal  Built on crypto for a closed strategy, run unchanged on 66 US symbols. Nothing trades until it clears lane A.
DThe fittingsplits, selection, tuning

D1Train and test splitchronological

Random partitioning places later observations in training and earlier ones in test. Returns are autocorrelated, so this leaks future information and inflates every score.

The yellow sliver is the gap. Without it the last rows studied still await outcomes landing inside the test window.

Single scoring of the held-out year  The final year is opened at the end and scored once. A setting chosen by looking at it has spent it, and it cannot be made unseen.

D2Hyperparameter sweepnine settings

Nine hyperparameter combinations, each scored on three walk-forward folds of the 4-hour panel, target being bars until the Supertrend reverses.

Fold variance exceeds configuration variance  A separate comparison of three feature representations spanned 0.094 bars. This is the argument for walk-forward scoring over a single split, and against ranking configurations on one fold.

D3Variable selectionelastic net

Ninety features against the available sample size admits overfitting. Retention is decided by an elastic-net penalty on the training split, before the model is fitted.

penalty
every coefficient is pushed toward zero; useless ones reach it
strength
chosen by cross-validation, then loosened by one standard error
kept
whatever still has a coefficient after that

Taking the loosest setting within one standard error of the best, rather than the best itself, deliberately keeps fewer inputs than the score alone would. The best-scoring setting is usually the one that has begun fitting the noise.

Selection confined to the training split  Choosing inputs is a decision, so it spends the data it is made on. Made on the blind year, it would spend that year, and the year cannot be made unseen again.
After-fee out-of-sample score decides  A penalty says which inputs a regression keeps. It does not say whether a family earns its place. That is settled by C2 and by the after-fee score in lane E.

1-hour panel, 6 Sep 2026. A ratio near 1 generalises. Above 1.1 it has memorised, and is thrown out.

Two defects in model selection and calibration  The winner was chosen on the unseen miss alone, never the ratio, crowning HistGBM at 1.840 where the rule rejects past 1.1. And every model treats rare wins as common, pushing its probabilities away from what this score measures: on 4-hour bars it misses by 0.4844 where answering "25.4 per cent, always" misses 0.4353. Nothing checks whether a stated 30 per cent happens 30 per cent of the time.

Figures quoted without opening the record Three figures on the previous version of this sheet were wrong because they were recalled rather than read. Open the file that produced the number, every time, including your own.

A render can succeed and still be wrong A page can build, exit cleanly, and report 0 of 6 cycles where there are 3. Compare the new render against the last one before believing it.

Overfit ratio inverted on two occasions The overfit ratio was written upside down, corrected on 7 September in one file, and found the same day still inverted in a second. A model reported as stable at 0.902 was really 1.109, which the rule rejects.

Broker equity differs from marked positions On 7 September, a market holiday, the broker reported $98,362 while the holdings at Friday's real closes came to $96,917. Value the positions yourself before reporting a number.
