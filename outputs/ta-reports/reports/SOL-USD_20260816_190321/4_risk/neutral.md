When the MACD histogram is at +0.22 and the signal line is still negative, we're in a **divergence state**, not a confirmed crossover. The Aggressive Analyst wants us to treat a divergence like it's a done deal. That's not analysis; that's hope dressed up as technical rigor.

The Aggressive Analyst presents this as tactical sophistication. I see it as multiplying operational risk across three separate decision points.
