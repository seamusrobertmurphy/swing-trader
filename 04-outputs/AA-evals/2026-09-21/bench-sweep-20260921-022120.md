One model at one setting, scored under seven resampling regimes against a single blind period. Two regimes keep time in order and five ignore it; the difference between a regime's claimed error and the blind error is what that regime leaks.

## What each configuration changes

**expanding**. The training window grows and each fold is scored on what follows. The house regime.

`n_estimators=150 max_depth=4 min_samples_leaf=200`

**rolling**. The training window slides, so old regimes drop out of the fit. Time stays in order.

`n_estimators=150 max_depth=4 min_samples_leaf=200`

**kfold**. Equal random blocks, each scored once. A row an hour after a training row is nearly the same observation.

`n_estimators=150 max_depth=4 min_samples_leaf=200`

**repeated-kfold**. The same, reshuffled and repeated ten times.

`n_estimators=150 max_depth=4 min_samples_leaf=200`

**leave-one-out**. Every scored row is fitted on all the others, so its neighbours on both sides are in training. Capped at 200 rows drawn at random, as bench_run.folds_of says.

`n_estimators=150 max_depth=4 min_samples_leaf=200`

**monte-carlo**. Repeated random splits at 75 per cent training.

`n_estimators=150 max_depth=4 min_samples_leaf=200`

**bootstrap**. Resample with replacement and score the third of rows left out of bag.

`n_estimators=150 max_depth=4 min_samples_leaf=200`
