## Reading

The replay reproduces the live book: on 16 October it held 49 of the account's 50 names. The 6 point gap between the as-traded replay and the account sits almost all in the first week, -4.44 points in the week to 23 August, when the live book ran on the rules of 18 August, before the cluster cap and the fund exclusion of 25 August. That week it held 37 of 49 names in one group of chips, memory and optics, and the replay, built on today's rules, did not. The eight weeks since add up to -1.94 points, which is what execution has cost under the current rules, including the 26 August rebalance filled in the opening auction. The strategy itself, traded on time with no cost, lost 0.93 per cent against the market's 0.13, and nearly all of that is the week to 18 October, when the replay fell 10.85 per cent against the market's 1.97.

Correction, the same evening. The replay bought on 18 August at that day's close, and momentum names fell 4.75 per cent that day (momentum-check-20261019-222230), so the replay skipped a fall the live book, filled at 15:39, mostly took. Part of the first week's 4.44 points is therefore that timing, not only the rules before 25 August, which were not replayed. The strategy's own shortfall also depends on weighting: with the live rule, which never trims a winner, it was 0.8 points; with every name reset to equal weight each week, as the August backtest assumed, the book fell 3.69 per cent from the 18 August close against +0.13 for SPY, 3.8 points behind.
