## Reading

On all 20 stocks the gate at 0.5 raised what every kept candle earned in all three presets, from +1.35 to +2.41, +0.22 to +0.37 and +0.75 to +1.16 per cent a trade, while keeping about 45 per cent of the test candles. The models' own top fifth changed little, +2.52 to +2.42 and +0.22 to +0.23, except Best on record, which rose from -0.42 to +0.45. The gate therefore chooses better stock entries by itself and the models add little on top of it. These are overlapping 20-day windows on one test year, 26 to 82 separate trades at 0.5, so the result is a lead for more testing rather than a default; on crypto the same gate lowered both four-hour presets.
