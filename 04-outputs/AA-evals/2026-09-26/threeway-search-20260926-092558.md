# Three-way search, 2026-09-26

Record `threeway-search-20260926-092558.json`, written by `03-inputs/threeway_search.py`. Six learners were fitted one coin at a time on 18 coins, 4h and 1d candles and horizons of 12 and 24 candles, 72 combinations in all. 32 combinations ran and gave 192 fits; 40 were left out. Money figures are per cent per trade after the 0.20 per cent round-trip cost, for the fifth of candles the model rated most bullish over bearish.

## Design

Every fit went through `demo_run.build_frame` and `demo_run.score`, the download, features and scoring a user's run uses, so research and live runs read the same Binance candles. The settings were those of the best 16 September three-way run (`04-outputs/AA-evals/2026-09-16/bench-3way-20260916-140850.json`), a break-even band of 1.0 per cent, 3 walk-forward folds and a final test year of 365 days. Models were ranked on the validation folds inside the training window. The test year was scored once and never used to choose.

## Left out

40 combinations left too few candles to fit, fewer than the 500 training and 100 test candles a run needs. Daily candles ran for BNB, BTC, ETH, XRP only. The house screen, at least 30 million USDT traded in the past 24 hours plus a volatility band, removes most daily candles of smaller coins; ADA kept 674 of 2,633, and the A1 volatility band of 0.015 to 0.071 of price then removed 280 more. On 4-hour candles ZEC, UNI, SUI, ENA, NEAR and DOT were left out the same way.

## Research against live

Research and live runs now share one pipeline, so their scores differ only by the candles each reads. The old preset, logistic regression on LINK and LTC 4-hour candles over 12, scored +1.23 per cent on the test period of `bench-3way-20260916-140850.json`, a basket of LINK, LTC and MATIC on the 25 MB research slice. On the demo's own candles it scored -1.15 per cent on LINK and +0.55 per cent on LTC, against -0.11 and -0.45 for every candle.

## Preset choice

The rule is unchanged, the highest after-cost return of the most confident fifth, read on validation. It chose LightGBM on XRP 1d candles over 24, at +27.89 per cent in validation and +9.07 on the test year against -3.40 for every candle. The test year held 142 candles whose 24-candle windows overlap, so it holds about 5 independent trades, and the top fifth fewer; the figure is suggestive, not established. The 4 largest validation figures are all daily cells, whose test years hold 135 to 154 candles, so the rule favours the cells with the least evidence.
