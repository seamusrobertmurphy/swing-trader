## Tuning and data

The app already runs a pre-registered tuning experiment. Each scan rotates through test slots, a market, candle size, outcome, horizon and learner, and `05-research/research/confirmed-best-rule.md` decides in advance when one may be called the best. It needs 30 non-overlapping basket trades whose 95 per cent confidence interval lies wholly above zero after cost, and buys that beat passes.

That rule is sound, but the horizons make it slow. A stock ticket is held 20 trading days, so 30 in a row take about 600 trading days, near two and a half years. A three-way crypto ticket is held 24 days, near two years. The crypto barrier presets hold for two days but buy almost never, so they will not reach 30 at their present rate either. Tuning the models further on past prices has been tested repeatedly since September and moved held-out error by about 2.6 per cent at best, while the choice of period moved it roughly forty times more than the choice of setting. More tuning of the same models on the same data is not where the answer is.

## Real money

The stock trader already has the switch. `03-inputs/alpaca_trade.py` aborts before any request unless the address is the paper one, or `LIVE_TRADING` is the exact word `true`. It places buys in dollar amounts, so a small account can hold all 50 names. Moving to real money takes five steps that only the account holder can take.

1. Open and fund a live Alpaca brokerage account, which requires identity checks.
2. Create live API keys in the Alpaca dashboard and store them under separate names from the paper keys.
3. Run the first live rebalance by hand, inside market hours, with `ALPACA_BASE_URL=https://api.alpaca.markets` and `LIVE_TRADING=true` set for that one command only.
4. Keep the scheduled tick on paper until the first live week is checked, so nothing trades real money unattended.
5. Compare the live fills with the paper fills using `03-inputs/alpaca_execution_report.py` after the first week.

The paper record is seven weeks long and the backtest's worst single month was -27.8 per cent, so a real account should be sized to survive a loss of that size.
