# Resampling regime sweep, regime-memoriser, 16 September 2026 06:05

The seven resampling regimes on a forest with unconstrained depth and one row a leaf, the most a forest can memorise, against the same blind period. The regime sweep on the incumbent measures the leak on a model too blunt to exploit it; this one measures it on a model that can.

Binance spot crypto, slice_4h_40k bars, 3 symbols (LINK/USDT, LTC/USDT, MATIC/USDT), 12,000 most recent in-sample rows. Label 2 ATR take-profit against 1 ATR stop within 12 bars. Split holds out the final 365 days with a label-horizon embargo, 3 expanding folds. No variable screen. Estimators RF, class weight none, rejecting above a 1.1 overfit ratio.

One panel read once, split at 2025-06-04 into 6,735 training rows and 1,286 blind. Every configuration is scored against those same rows, so a difference between two rows is the configuration and nothing else.

## What each configuration changes

**expanding** &mdash; The training window grows and each fold is scored on what follows. The house regime.

`n_estimators=100 max_depth=0 min_samples_leaf=1`

**rolling** &mdash; The training window slides, so old regimes drop out of the fit. Time stays in order.

`n_estimators=100 max_depth=0 min_samples_leaf=1`

**kfold** &mdash; Equal random blocks, each scored once. A row an hour after a training row is nearly the same observation.

`n_estimators=100 max_depth=0 min_samples_leaf=1`

**repeated-kfold** &mdash; The same, reshuffled and repeated ten times.

`n_estimators=100 max_depth=0 min_samples_leaf=1`

**leave-one-out** &mdash; Every scored row is fitted on all the others, so its neighbours on both sides are in training. Capped at 200 rows drawn at random, as bench_run.folds_of says.

`n_estimators=100 max_depth=0 min_samples_leaf=1`

**monte-carlo** &mdash; Repeated random splits at 75 per cent training.

`n_estimators=100 max_depth=0 min_samples_leaf=1`

**bootstrap** &mdash; Resample with replacement and score the third of rows left out of bag.

`n_estimators=100 max_depth=0 min_samples_leaf=1`
