What the concentration bar means

The biggest group of stocks that move together is 18 of the 50 holdings, worth 32.4% of the money. Your rules cap any such group at 20%.

Momentum concentrates on purpose: last year’s winners are last year’s winning industry. That is why the book can lose six percent in a week the market barely moves, and win it back the next day. Both are the same fact.
